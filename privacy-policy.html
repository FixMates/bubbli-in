Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Overview

Bubbli Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website bubbli.in, interact with our web applications, or use our digital development and workflow automation services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways:

  • Personal Data: Name, business name, email address (support@bubbli.in), phone number, and WhatsApp contact information provided when submitting inquiry forms or booking consultations.
  • Project Data: Business requirements, logos, media, menu items, price lists, and domain credentials submitted for website construction.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, operating system, IP address, referral URLs, and interactions with our interactive calculators and demos.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To build, deploy, configure, and maintain your custom website or web applications.
  • To provide direct customer service, technical updates, and project consultations via phone, email, or WhatsApp.
  • To generate accurate quotations and timelines tailored to your industry requirements.
  • To maintain website security and optimize user experience across our digital assets.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement high-grade encryption, secure server architecture, and SSL protocols to safeguard client data. We never sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information or business databases to third parties.

5. Cookies and Web Beacons

Our website may use standard functional cookies and analytics tracking (such as Google Analytics) to improve navigation performance and understand user engagement. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings.
